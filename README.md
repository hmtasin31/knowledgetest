# 🎓 জ্ঞান পরীক্ষা | Knowledge Test



## 📖 About The Project

**জ্ঞান পরীক্ষা (Knowledge Test)** is a beautifully designed, interactive Bengali quiz application built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The platform allows users to test their knowledge across 12 different subjects through randomized questions, interactive answer options, real-time scoring, and a countdown timer.

After completing a quiz, users can view their performance, share their results, and download a personalized certificate of achievement.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **12 Different Subjects** – Explore a variety of knowledge-based topics.
- 📝 **Randomized Questions** – Get a fresh set of questions for every attempt.
- 🔀 **Shuffled Options** – Answer choices are randomized.
- ⏱️ **Countdown Timer** – Complete each quiz within 2 minutes.
- 📊 **Live Score Tracking** – Track your score while answering questions.
- 🎯 **Instant Feedback** – See correct and incorrect answers immediately.
- 🏆 **Achievement System** – Special celebrations for different score levels.
- 🎓 **Certificate Generator** – Create and download personalized certificates in PNG format.
- 📤 **Share Results** – Share your quiz performance with others.
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** – Interactive audio feedback with a mute option.
- 💾 **Question History** – Local storage helps avoid repeating recently answered questions.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Enjoy the experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- 🇧🇩 **Bengali Interface** – Designed primarily for Bengali-speaking users.

## 🛠️ Built With

| Technology | Purpose |

## 🎮 How To Use

1. Open the Knowledge Test application.
2. Click the **Start Quiz** button.
3. Enter your name for the certificate.
4. Choose your preferred quiz subject.
5. Answer the questions before the timer runs out.
6. View your final score and performance.
7. Generate and download your achievement certificate.
8. Share your results with friends!

## 🌐 Deployment

You can host this project for free using **GitHub Pages**.

1. Upload the project files to a GitHub repository.
2. Open your repository's **Settings**.
3. Navigate to **Pages**.
4. Select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder.
6. Click **Save**.

Your application will be available at:

```text
https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPOSITORY/
```
